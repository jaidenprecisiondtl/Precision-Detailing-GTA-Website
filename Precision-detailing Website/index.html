About

Detailing that comes to you.

Precision Detailing started with a simple idea. Nobody wants to give up a Saturday sitting in a waiting room while someone else cleans their car. So we brought the shop to the driveway. Same equipment, same standard, none of the drop-off.

Every job is done by hand, one car at a time. No rushing through a queue, no cutting corners on the parts you cannot see. If it is not right, it does not get handed back.

Outside of detailing, I have spent six years coaching youth sports here in Brampton and was recognized by the City of Brampton for that work. Same principle carries over. Show up on time, do the job properly, treat people well.
